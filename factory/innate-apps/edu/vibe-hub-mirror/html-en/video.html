Video

You might say

Put a video on the page that people can play with one click.

Media with moving images and a duration that plays on a page·Video can demonstrate a product action or teach a course, such as “Create a project in two minutes.” The poster should state the topic and length, and the player needs captions, sound controls, and a clear failure state.
