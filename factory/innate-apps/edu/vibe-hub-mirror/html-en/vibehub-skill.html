Function one · Rewrite

Turn everyday language into a request that is ready to send

The Skill preserves your intent and adds only the terms that help. It will not invent a framework, parameters, or implementation details.

What you said

Change the button color when I move the mouse over it, and make it react when I press it.

Ready to send

Add a Hover state and an Active state to the button, then use a Transition to make the visual change feel smooth.

Function two · Proactive hints

You do not have to ask, “What is this called?”

Whenever your wording is ambiguous or missing the right name, the Skill surfaces the one to three terms that actually matter and explains them in context—before you have to ask.

You said

I want this setting to take effect as soon as I change it. I do not want another Save button.

VibeHub suggests

A Switchis usually a good fit here: it works for a setting with two states that takes effect immediately. If people need to adjust several options and confirm them together, this is not the right pattern.

  • The first mention links straight to VibeHub
  • The explanation stays tied to the task at hand
  • Only one to three genuinely useful terms at a time
