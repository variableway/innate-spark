Single Page

You might say

Put everything on one long page so people can just scroll through it.

Arrange a complete story in sections down one scrolling page·A single-page layout guides visitors through a sequence such as value, proof, features, pricing, and action. It works when the story has one main goal. Clear sections and anchor navigation help, but important content should not depend on scroll effects.
