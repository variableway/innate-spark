Human-in-the-loop

You might say

The AI can research the itinerary and flights, but it must ask me before paying or issuing a ticket.

Pausing an AI workflow before a critical decision or high-risk action so a person can review, approve, reject, or correct it·A travel agent may search and compare flights automatically, but before payment it should show the dates, passenger, total, and change rules and wait for approval. Human-in-the-loop adds decision points where they matter rather than making a person watch every step.

Human-in-the-loop keeps low-risk research automatic and places a human decision before the high-risk action.

What makes a useful human decision point

Pause before the action: Search and comparison can run automatically. Charging, ticketing, publishing, or deletion must be blocked before the high-risk action occurs.

Show enough information to decide: An approval cannot be only “continue?” The interface should show the object, scope, cost, rules, and expected consequence.

Return the decision to the same run: Approval resumes from the pause, rejection stops it, and corrections return with human feedback without restarting the whole task.

What people often confuse

A guardrail can block automatically by rule; human-in-the-loop gives a key judgment to a person. Auth decides who has permission, while HITL decides when a human decision is required. The term is also used in data labeling and training; this page focuses on approval and correction during an agent run.
