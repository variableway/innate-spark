Back to Top Button

You might say

After someone scrolls way down, show a button in the bottom right that takes them back to the top.

Return quickly to the top of a long page·A back-to-top control is useful after substantial scrolling, especially when important navigation sits at the top. Keep it out of the way and reveal it only after the user has moved far enough. It does not solve a poorly structured page.
