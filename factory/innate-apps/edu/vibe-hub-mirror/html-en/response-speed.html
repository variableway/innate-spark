TTFT / TPS

You might say

Why do some AI tools take forever to show the first word while others answer right away?

Measures of time to first output and of token generation speed after output begins·TTFT measures time from sending a request to receiving the first token. TPS measures tokens produced each second afterward. A quick first token does not guarantee a short total response time.

Separate first output, speed, and total time

Wait for TTFT: TTFT runs from send to the first text token.

Then watch TPS: TPS is tokens per second after generation begins, not transactions per second.

Then measure total time: A longer answer can still take longer to finish even when it begins quickly.

What people often confuse

A short TTFT does not mean a fast full response; generation speed and output length still matter.
