Social Proof

You might say

Add a row of customer logos and a line that says more than 100,000 teams use us.

Support a product claim with evidence from real use·Social proof can include customer logos, measured results, reviews, certifications, or case studies. Choose evidence that supports the claim next to it and state the source or context. A wall of unverified logos is weaker than one relevant, specific example.
