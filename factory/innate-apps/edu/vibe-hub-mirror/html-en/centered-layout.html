Centered Column

You might say

Keep the content in a narrow column with space on both sides instead of stretching it across the whole screen, like a blog.

Keep one narrow content column centered on the page·A centered column focuses attention on reading or one simple task such as signing in, composing a post, or changing settings. Set a comfortable maximum width and keep supporting actions nearby. It is not suitable for dense dashboards that need side-by-side comparison.
Content columncentered and limited to 600~720px

When to use it

  • Article or essay
    ↔ 60–75 characters per line
  • Sign-in and account setup
    Content column
  • Focused form
    oil-oil••••••••
    Sign in
  • Simple settings or editor
    Body text needs a readable size and line height for long-form reading.

When NOT to use it

  • Place wide tables in a narrow column
  • Center-align long body paragraphs
  • Let the column grow too wide on large screens
    Sign-in card
  • Use excessive empty margins when more context is needed
