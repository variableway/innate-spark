Card Grid

You might say

Show the content as cards in a grid, with more columns on wide screens and wrapping on narrow ones.

Arrange similar cards in a responsive grid·A card grid helps people browse products, templates, articles, or projects with a shared visual structure. Define how columns change with width, keep card content comparable, and avoid forcing every card to the same height when content differs greatly.
