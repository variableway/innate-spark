Editorial

You might say

Lay it out like a fashion magazine, with images and text woven together.

Use publication-style typography, imagery, and pacing to tell a story·Editorial web design borrows from magazines through expressive headlines, considered image crops, captions, columns, and varied pacing. It works when the content deserves sustained reading. The layout should adapt without turning mobile into a squeezed print spread.

Design Principles
  • Let the story determine the visual rhythm
  • Pair expressive headlines with readable body text
  • Use images, captions, and pull quotes as real editorial material
  • Control line length and whitespace for long reading
  • Recompose the hierarchy for each screen size

When to use it

  • Magazine and culture site
    Thirty Years of a Tea CakeBrand Story · Issue 12
  • Brand story
    "Slowness is a kind of ability."
  • Long-form case study
    Spend a Week in the Mountains
  • Travel, fashion, or architecture feature
    Annual special issue2026

When NOT to use it

  • Use dramatic layouts around thin content
    Magazine-style layouts make a data back office harder to scan.
  • Make body text secondary to decoration
    Customer-service workspace (drop-cap version)High-frequency work interfaces should prioritize scanability and processing efficiency.
  • Copy a print spread without responsive behavior
    The Buy button is hidden at the end of the third column.Conversion pages: the CTA must not get lost in the layout.
  • Mix typefaces with no assigned editorial roles
    Keeping three columns on a phone makes lines too narrow.

Use a serif display face, such as Georgia or an appropriate CJK serif, to add editorial character and visual weight.
The text is sans-serif to keep it easy to read, and two types of fonts create hierarchy.
Narrow columns and short lines are the key to reading long articles. Remember to collapse to one column on small screens.
Use at the start of a suitable long article to introduce a paragraph. It does not suit every paragraph or language.
Create magazine-style editorial context with issue numbers, page numbers and column titles
