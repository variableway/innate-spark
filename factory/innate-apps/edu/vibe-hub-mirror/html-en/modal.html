Modal

You might say

Click the button to show a dialog in the middle of the screen and dim the background.

Pause the page for one focused decision or task·A modal appears above the page when the user must review or complete a focused task before continuing. It needs a clear title, a safe way to close, sensible keyboard focus, and buttons that state the result. Long or complex work is usually better on its own page.
