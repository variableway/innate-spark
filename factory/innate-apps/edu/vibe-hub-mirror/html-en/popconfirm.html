Popconfirm

You might say

On delete, ask 'Are you sure?' next to the button instead of opening a big dialog.

Ask for confirmation next to the action that caused it·A popconfirm is a small confirmation panel anchored to a button or link. It works for a simple, local action with a short explanation. Use a Modal when the risk is high, the explanation is longer, or more information must be entered.
